⭐ Capstone Project Retail Analytics

Retail Sales Performance Analysis — Independent Capstone

Problem Statement

Retail teams often run discount campaigns without knowing which discount bands, products, or regions are quietly destroying margin — decisions get made on gut feel rather than the numbers hidden inside thousands of order rows.

Solution

Analysed 9,994 retail orders end-to-end — SQL (joins, CTEs, window functions), Python EDA, and a 3-page Power BI dashboard with custom DAX measures — to pinpoint exactly where discounting was eroding profit and by how much.

Identified that orders discounted above 40% lost 77% margin on average, and isolated a single product-region combination responsible for $21,900 in losses — translating raw transactional data into prioritised, actionable business recommendations.
